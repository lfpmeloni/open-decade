# Tax guidance boundary and module specification

Version 1.0.0. Reviewed 2026-10-09; review due 2027-01-09. Applies to US
federal, Germany, UK, France, Spain, Italy and Netherlands modules. Source:
[authorized scope](../docs/roadmap.md). The installed 2025 document modules below provide conditional source references
and collection questions. No liability or filing decision is provided.

Route only after collecting relevant country, tax year, residence uncertainty,
foreign accounts, disposals, investment income, wrappers and retirement documents.
Keep potentially applicable countries separate. Citizenship, residence and an
account's country are different facts; do not infer one from another. Unsupported
cross-border, trust, estate, business or state/local questions become explicit
professional-review questions, never guessed rules.

Each country/year guide must cite dated primary sources, identify the publisher,
applicability and review deadline, list documents and unresolved questions, and
mark stale content. Questionnaire answers and document references belong in the
vault. Handoff packages distinguish confirmed documents from missing ones. A
checklist item is not a determination that a return or form must be filed.

Example: a user with German residence and a US account gets separate applicability
questions. The application must not multiply balances by an assumed tax rate,
propose quantified tax savings, decide a filing obligation or submit a return.
Foreign-source summaries, reference-product calculators and another jurisdiction's
thresholds cannot supply authoritative rules.
